Jackson Walker

I studied UCSB with a BS in Financial Mathematics and Statistics, then later finished the Master of Financial Engineering at Berkeley Haas School of Business.

I work in data science and applied ML, mostly in finance. A lot of my recent work has been around shipping practical LLM systems inside banking workflows, where the interesting part is usually less about the model and more about the surrounding system: retrieval, controls, evaluation, and getting the thing to behave in production.

Outside of work I build small internet projects, analytical tools, and whatever else seems worth understanding from the inside. Some of that ends up here, including LovelyWords.co, a writing tool, plus a stack of experiments in stats, scraping, simulations, and web apps. This site is mostly a notebook with better navigation.
