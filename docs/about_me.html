Jackson Walker

I studied UCSB with a BS in Financial Mathematics and Statistics, then later finished the Master of Financial Engineering at Berkeley Haas School of Business.

I am an Associate Director at Moody's, where I work on data science, applied ML/AI, and agentic LLM systems. The interesting part is usually less about the model and more about the surrounding system: retrieval, controls, evaluation, and getting the thing to behave in production.

Outside of work I build small tools, analytical projects, and whatever else seems worth understanding from the inside. Most of what ends up here is some combination of stats, scraping, simulations, and web apps. This site is mostly a notebook with better navigation.
